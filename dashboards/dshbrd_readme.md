# Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an overview of course continuation patterns and student-level information. It consists of two pages designed to support course performance analysis and student segmentation.

## 1. Course Continuation

**Objective:** Analyze how long students continue accessing their courses and compare continuation patterns across courses.

**Key elements:**
- Funnel visualization showing the number and percentage of students reaching each course month.
- Course-level breakdown of continuation metrics.
- Monthly continuation indicators.
- Course selection filter.

**Analytical value:**

The funnel helps identify how student numbers change over the course duration and at which stages student continuation declines most significantly.

Course-level comparisons make it possible to identify differences in continuation patterns and highlight courses that may require further investigation.

## 2. Student Information

**Objective:** Identify and segment students based on their course participation and learning activity.

**Key elements:**
- Student-level table with course and enrollment information.
- Student status and enrollment wave.
- City information.
- Number of completed homework assignments.
- Last course month reached.
- Filters for student status, course, enrollment wave, city, homework activity, and course continuation.

**Analytical value:**

The page supports targeted student segmentation. For example, users can identify active students who have not reached a particular course month and filter the resulting group by course, enrollment wave, city, or homework activity.

The resulting student list can support further investigation and help identify groups for potential sales or student support activities.

## Conclusion

The dashboard combines course-level continuation analysis with detailed student information.

It helps:
- Monitor student continuation patterns across courses.
- Compare course performance.
- Identify stages where student continuation declines.
- Segment students for further analysis and targeted communication.

**Note:** Course continuation is measured using available course access and enrollment data. It does not represent confirmed subscription renewals or payments.
